# Seasonal Onion Price Analysis in Indian Agricultural Markets

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing historical onion prices in Indian agricultural markets (mandis) to identify seasonal price patterns, regional differences, and price volatility.

The project aims to use data analytics to understand how onion prices vary across different months, years, and markets and to generate evidence-based insights for agribusiness stakeholders.

## Objectives

- Analyze historical onion prices across selected Indian mandis.
- Identify seasonal patterns in onion prices.
- Compare prices across selected states and markets.
- Measure price volatility across different periods and markets.
- Examine the relationship between market arrivals and onion prices.
- Explore the influence of seasonal and weather-related factors where data is available.
- Develop practical, evidence-based insights for agribusiness stakeholders.

## Research Questions

1. In which months are onion prices historically higher or lower?
2. How consistent are seasonal price patterns across different years?
3. How do onion prices differ across selected states and markets?
4. How volatile are onion prices across months, years, and markets?
5. Is there an observable relationship between market arrivals and prices?
6. What seasonal or weather-related factors appear to be associated with price changes?

## Limitations

- Historical price patterns do not guarantee future prices.
- Public datasets may contain missing or inconsistent records.
- Factors such as storage, transportation, quality, and local demand may not be fully captured.
- Observed relationships between variables do not necessarily establish causation.
